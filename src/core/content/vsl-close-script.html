They watched the video

This lead has already sat through the pitch. Do not re-pitch it at them. Your whole job on this call is to find out what they thought, clear whatever is in the way, and ask for the yes.
1 · Opener
You: "Hi, you alright? Quick one: is that [owner_first], from [business_name]?"
Wait for the yes before you say anything else. Same as always.
You: "Brilliant. It's Elsie, I sent you over that short video about where you're showing up on Google. I saw you'd watched it, so I thought I'd give you a quick ring. What did you make of it?"
Say "I saw you'd watched it" plainly. It is true, they know it is true, and it is the reason this call is not cold. Then stop talking. The question at the end is the whole opener, do not follow it with a pitch.
2 · Let them react
Them: "Yeah, it was interesting" / "Yeah I saw it"
You: "Good. What stood out, the ranking bit or the reviews bit?"
Let them talk and do not fill the silence. Whatever problem they name in their own words is the one you close on later, so listen for it and use their phrasing back at them. Agree with it before you move.
Them: "I only half watched it" / "I skipped through it"
You: "No bother. The short version: you're sat behind [competitors_ahead] other [trade_plural] in [town], and the thing separating you from them is reviews. That's the gap we close. Does that sound like something worth sorting?"
Only give the summary if they admit they did not really watch it. If they watched it, giving them the summary anyway tells them you were not listening.
3 · Clear what is in the way
Quick reference only. The full approved list is in the Objections tab on the right of this screen. Handle it, then go straight back to the close below, do not wander.
"I need to think about it"
You: "Course. What's the bit you want to think about, is it the money or whether it'll actually work for you?"
"How much is it again?"
You: "It's the same numbers you saw on the video, and it's a pound to start today. You'll see it working before you're paying properly for it."
"I already ask for reviews myself"
You: "Most lads do, and it's always the same story: you remember on a good week and forget on a busy one. This asks every single customer, every time, without you touching it. That's the difference between where you are and where you want to be."
"I'll have a look and get back to you"
You: "I know how that goes, you'll get busy and it'll be next month. It takes a minute while I'm on the phone with you. Shall we just get you on it?"
Anything else, open the Objections tab. Do not improvise a new answer.
4 · Close
You: "So is that something you want to move forward with?"
Ask it, then say nothing at all until they answer.
Them: "Yeah, go on then"
You: "Perfect. I'm texting you the link now. Tap Subscribe, pop your details in, and I'll get you onboarded while we're on the phone."
Use the Send subscribe link button on the left of this screen and send it while they are still on the line. Stay on the call and walk them through it, do not hang up and hope. A yes that fills the form in is worth ten that get a link after the call.
Them: "Not right now"
No hard close, they have already given you their time twice. Set the outcome in the pipeline dropdown as normal (Interested, Nurturing) so they land back in the follow-up list, and their card on the video board carries that outcome for whoever picks them up next.

Set them to Booked in the pipeline dropdown. Then ask for their customer list, that is the thing that gets their first wave of reviews landing within days.
