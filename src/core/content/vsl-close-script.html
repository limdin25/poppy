They watched the video

Warm call, and a short one. They have already had the pitch, so do not pitch it again. Read the blue lines aloud, top to bottom. Four beats: who is that, why you are ringing, any questions, ask for the yes. Grey lines are notes for you only. If they push back, open the amber button on that line, then carry on down.
Who is that
YOU: "Hi, is that [owner_first]?"
Just that. No "you alright", no "quick one", nothing clever. You are ringing someone who asked to hear from you by watching your video, so ring like it. No owner name on the card and it reads the business name instead, which is still right.
✓ "Yeah, speaking", keep reading ↓
Someone else answers obj
YOU: "Oh sorry, is the owner about? It's about the video I sent over about their Google reviews, they've had a look at it."
Do not pitch whoever answered. Get the owner, or get the best time to ring back.
Why you are ringing
YOU: "It's Elsie here. I'm just calling you back about that video I sent you over, the one about your Google reviews. I could see you'd watched it, so I thought I'd give you a ring."
Then stop. Do not add a pitch onto the end of it. Let it land.
✓ They say "oh yeah" / "right", keep reading ↓

One line only, then straight to the close. Do not replay the video at them.
"Who is this? What video?" obj
YOU: "Elsie, we sent you a short video about where [business_name] shows up on Google and how the reviews stack up against the others near you. It went to this number."
Say it plainly and move on. They watched it, the page says so.
Any questions
YOU: "So I was just wondering, have you got any questions about it?"
Say it and go quiet. If they ask something, answer it straight and short, then go to the close. If they say no, no questions, go to the close now. Do not fill the gap, do not start explaining the video back to them.

Anything else they ask obj
YOU: "Good question, let me check that properly and come straight back to you."
The approved answers are in the Objections tab on the right. Never invent one, and never promise a position on Google.
Close
YOU: "So is that something you'd like to get started with then, and get you some reviews coming in?"
Ask it, then say nothing at all until they answer. First one to speak loses.

Whatever they name, answer it in one line and ask for the yes again.

One push, said warmly. If it is still no, take the outcome and leave it.
"Not right now" obj
YOU: "No problem at all. I'll leave it with you, the link's on your phone whenever you want it."
Leave it. They have given you their time twice already. Set the outcome in the dropdown (Interested, Nurturing) and they come back round in the follow-ups.

Hit Send subscribe link on the left and stay on the line while they do it. A yes that fills the form in beats ten that get a link afterwards.
