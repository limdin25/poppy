They watched the video

Warm call, and a short one. They have already had the pitch, so do not pitch it again. Four beats: say who you are, say why you are ringing, ask if they have any questions, ask for the yes. Do not go round the houses.
1 · Who is that
You: "Hi, is that [owner_first]?"
Just that. No "you alright", no "quick one", nothing clever. You are ringing someone who asked to hear from you by watching your video, so ring like it. No owner name on the card? Then: "Hi, is that [business_name]?"
2 · Why you are ringing
You: "It's Elsie here. I'm just calling you back about that video I sent you over, the one about your Google reviews. I could see you'd watched it, so I thought I'd give you a ring."
Then stop. Do not add a pitch onto the end of it. Let it land.
3 · Any questions
You: "So I was just wondering, have you got any questions about it?"
Say it and go quiet. If they ask something, answer it straight and short, then go to the close. If they say no, no questions, go to the close now. Do not fill the gap, do not start explaining the video back to them.
4 · Close
You: "So is that something you'd like to get started with then, and get you some reviews coming in?"
Ask it, then say nothing at all until they answer. First one to speak loses.
Them: "Yeah, go on then"
You: "Perfect. I'm texting you the link now. Tap Subscribe, pop your details in, and I'll get you sorted while we're on the phone."
Hit Send subscribe link on the left and stay on the line while they do it. A yes that fills the form in beats ten that get a link afterwards.
Them: "Not right now"
Leave it. They have given you their time twice already. Set the outcome in the dropdown (Interested, Nurturing) and they come back round in the follow-ups.
If they push back
Answer in one line and go straight back to the close. Every extra sentence is a new thing for them to object to. The full approved list is in the Objections tab on the right.
"I need to think about it"
You: "Course. What is it you want to think about, the money or whether it'll work?"
"How much is it again?"
You: "Same as it says on the video, and it's a pound to start today. You'll see it working before you're paying properly for it."
"I already ask for reviews myself"
You: "Everyone does on a good week and forgets on a busy one. This asks every customer, every time, without you touching it."
"I'll have a look and get back to you"
You: "You'll get busy and it'll be next month. It takes a minute while I'm here. Shall we just do it?"
"I didn't really watch it" / "I skipped through it"
You: "No bother. You're sat behind [competitors_ahead] other [trade_plural] in [town], and the thing between you and them is reviews. That's what this fixes. Worth a go?"

Set them to Booked in the dropdown, then ask for their customer list. That is what gets their first reviews landing within days.
