1. Availability and authority

When would it be ready to let? Are you the owner, or managing it for the owner?

Whole-property studios and one-bedroom homes only. Confirm the advert: [sa_address], [sa_beds] bedrooms, [sa_agency]. Do not imply you are looking to rent it.

2. Offer the report
We help owners manage short stays through Airbnb. I can send you a clearly labelled area estimate, showing the assumed property details, estimated earnings and costs compared with your rent. We can refine it with your full property details. Would that be useful?

Figures are estimates, not guaranteed income.

3. Send and confirm receipt
Can I confirm the full postcode and bathroom count? What mobile number or email should I send the report to?

4. Review the numbers
Have you had a chance to read the report? What did you think?

Software is free in month one, then £29 a month for UK leads or $29 a month outside the UK from month two. Booking revenue includes guest-paid cleaning. Actual cleaning, platform fees and property costs are separate. VAT registration is pending; billing remains 9% for now.

5. Readiness and next step
If you would like to go ahead, can I ask a few questions so we can give you a clear setup checklist?

Check availability, authority and permissions, furniture and essentials, photos, access, cleaning and the owner's Airbnb account. Ask what is missing, the setup budget and completion dates.
