Hostunico sales guide

Help them decide. Ask one question, then listen.

1. Availability
Hi, this is Pedro here. I saw your [sa_type] in [sa_city] at [sa_rent] on SpareRoom. Is it still available?
Have you got one minute for me to explain why I called?

Wait for permission. Whole-property studios and one-bedroom homes only. Confirm the advert: [sa_address], [sa_beds] bedrooms, [sa_agency]. Say Hostunico if asked. Do not imply you are looking to rent it.

2. Offer the report
We manage properties for short-term lets through Airbnb. Would you like me to send you an earnings report so you can have a look and see if it is something you would like to explore?

If the report is ready, use its actual monthly figure and asking-rent comparison before offering to send it. Never invent a number or claim an unfinished report is ready. Figures are estimates, not guaranteed income. Answer questions if asked; keep the opening short.

3. Send and confirm receipt
Can I confirm the full postcode and bathroom count? What mobile number or email should I send the report to?

Wait for Report ready. Press Send report by SMS only after they agree.

I am sending it now. Could you check whether you received the link? Want me to walk you through what onboarding looks like?
4. Review the numbers
Have you had a chance to read the report? What did you think?

Management fee: 9% + VAT. Software is free in month one, then £29 a month for UK leads or $29 a month outside the UK from month two, on top of management.

5. Readiness and next step
If you would like to go ahead, can I ask a few questions so we can give you a clear setup checklist?
When would it be ready to let?

Check authority and permissions, furniture and essentials, photos, access, cleaning and the owner's Airbnb account. Ask what is missing, the setup budget and completion dates. Send the agreed checklist and arrange the agreement and login through onboarding. Confirm launch only after the checks.
