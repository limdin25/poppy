# Hey Elsie — Sales Process

## Pricing Model (Final)

| Event | Cost |
|---|---|
| Sign up | **£0** |
| AI answers calls/messages | **Free** |
| AI books a job | **£20** |
| Monthly cap | **£189** |
| Card captured | **At first invoice (end of billing cycle)** |

No setup fee. No activation credit. No monthly fee. Pay per job only.

---

## Acquisition Channel

**Cold call → WhatsApp demo → instant setup**

No cold SMS (illegal under PECR for sole traders).
No cold WhatsApp (same rules).
Cold calling is legal if TPS-checked.

---

## Daily Workflow (VA)

```
1. VA cold calls 100 tradesmen (TPS-checked list)
   ↓
2. ~30 answer
   ↓
3. ~15 say "yeah, WhatsApp me"
   ↓
4. VA sends 60-sec demo video + one-liner on WhatsApp
   ↓
5. ~5-8 reply interested
   ↓
6. VA runs instant setup (5 mins on WhatsApp)
   ↓
7. ~2-3 sign up that day
```

One follow-up WhatsApp 2 days later to non-responders. No chasing beyond that.

## The Cold Call Script

> "Hi [Name], I'm Hugo from Hey Elsie. Quick one — when you
> miss a call because you're on a job, what happens to that
> lead? ... Elsie answers for you, knows your prices, books
> them into your diary. £20 a job, nothing if she doesn't
> book. Can I WhatsApp you a quick 60-second video showing
> how it works?"

---

## After They Say "I'm Interested" (WhatsApp Setup Flow)

### Step 1: Instant setup (5 mins)

VA sends:

> "Brilliant! Let's get you live now — takes 5 minutes.
>
> I just need 3 things:
> 1. What services do you offer + rough prices?
> 2. What are your working hours?
> 3. Dial this on your phone: **61*[Elsie number]*11*30#
>    (sends missed calls to Elsie after 30 seconds)"

### Step 2: VA enters details (3 mins)

While tradesman dials the forwarding code, VA:
- Creates the business in the admin dashboard
- Enters services, prices, hours
- Elsie is now live

### Step 3: Test call (1 min)

VA sends:

> "Done! Try it now — call your own number from another
> phone, let it ring 30 seconds, and Elsie will pick up.
> Have a listen"

### Step 4: Contract (after setup, not before)

VA sends:

> "Here's the simple agreement — just confirms £20/job,
> £189 cap, cancel anytime: [DocuSign link]
>
> No rush on that. Elsie's already live for you."

Contract is sent AFTER setup so momentum isn't killed.

### Step 5: WhatsApp channel (optional, Day 2+)

Only if they want Elsie on WhatsApp too:

> "Want Elsie to handle your WhatsApp messages too?
> Open this link on your phone and scan the QR code:
> [Unipile auth link]"

## Onboarding Timeline

```
Cold call (30 sec pitch)
    ↓ "yeah WhatsApp me"
Demo video on WhatsApp (60 secs)
    ↓ "looks good, let's do it"
Setup on WhatsApp (5 mins)
    ↓
Test call (hear Elsie answer)
    ↓
Contract link sent (sign whenever)
    ↓
LIVE — first missed call → Elsie answers → job booked → £20
```

Total time from "I'm interested" to live: **under 10 minutes**.

## VA Requirements

- Needs: clear English, friendly, can follow a script
- Handles: cold calls, WhatsApp demos, setup, one follow-up
- Hugo trains Week 1, VA is autonomous by Week 2

## Rules

- Never cold SMS or cold WhatsApp (illegal under PECR)
- Always TPS-check numbers before calling
- Contract is sent AFTER setup, not before
- One follow-up max, then move on
- Don't push WhatsApp channel on Day 1 — phone is the core product
