### Key Gaps Hey Elsie Can Exploit

1. **No one owns "AI receptionist + WhatsApp + Instagram + booking" for UK SMBs.** Phone-focused competitors don't handle WhatsApp or Instagram. Messaging platforms don't answer phones. GoHighLevel does everything but is built for agencies.

2. **UK market is wide open.** ARROW is the only AI-first UK competitor and they only do phone. Moneypenny is human-only and expensive. There is no UK-based multichannel AI receptionist at an affordable price.

3. **"AI steps in only when you don't respond" is unique positioning.** Every competitor either answers everything (full AI takeover) or requires manual responses.

4. **WhatsApp is critical in the UK and ignored by US competitors.** 75%+ penetration. Smith.ai, Ruby, Rosie, Goodcall all ignore it.

5. **Instagram DM handling is almost nonexistent in the receptionist category.** Only ManyChat and GoHighLevel handle Instagram DMs, and neither positions as a receptionist.

6. **Pricing gap between cheap phone-only ($25-49) and expensive all-in-one ($250+).** Hey Elsie at £29-99/mo with multichannel sits in the sweet spot.

### Why Drop SMS

- UK SMS volumes collapsed from 151 billion/year (2012) to ~28 billion — a 73%+ decline (Ofcom)
- WhatsApp has 41-42 million UK users and dominates personal messaging
- You cannot intercept inbound SMS on a business owner's personal mobile — no API, no carrier feature, no legal way
- You'd need a separate Twilio number, but UK consumers don't text businesses
- SMS costs ~$0.04/message outbound. Adds cost for a channel nobody uses
- The only remaining UK SMS use case is outbound notifications, which WhatsApp template messages handle better

### Why Add Webchat Widget

- 20% average increase in website conversions
- 73% customer satisfaction (vs 61% email, 44% phone)
- 2.8x more likely to purchase vs non-chat visitors
- Near-zero marginal cost (AI inference only ~£0.003-0.01/response)
- Can hand off to WhatsApp for ongoing conversation
- Setup: business owner pastes a script tag on their website

### Why Add Facebook Messenger

- Already available through Unipile (minimal new code)
- Many UK service businesses have a Facebook Page as primary web presence
- Tradesmen get inquiries through Facebook Marketplace and page messages
- 24-hour messaging window (same as WhatsApp)

### SMS vs WhatsApp in the UK

**SMS is essentially dead for inbound business enquiries in the UK.**

- SMS volumes fell from 151 billion (2012 peak) to ~28 billion — a 73% decline over a decade (Ofcom)
- WhatsApp is used by 75% of UK online adults (Ofcom 2024), up from 68% in 2022
- UK consumers default to WhatsApp for person-to-person and increasingly person-to-business messaging
- Tradespeople report customers who don't call will WhatsApp — almost never SMS
- SMS is now primarily outbound by businesses (appointment reminders, 2FA, marketing)

### The 5-Minute Rule

**MIT/InsideSales.com study (Professor James Oldroyd, 2007-2011):**
- Contacting a lead within **5 minutes** = **21x more likely** to qualify vs 30 minutes
- 5 minutes vs 10 minutes: **4x more likely** to qualify
- After 30 minutes: lead qualification drops **100x** compared to 5 minutes
- Study analysed 15,000+ leads across multiple industries

**Harvard Business Review (2011):**
- Audited 2,241 companies — average response time was **42 hours**
- Only 37% responded within 1 hour
- Companies responding within 1 hour were **7x more likely** to have meaningful conversation

**Lead Connect (2020):**
- **78% of customers buy from the company that responds first**
- 30-50% of sales go to the vendor that responds first

**Vendasta (2023):**
- 55% of businesses take **5+ days** to respond — or never respond at all
- Businesses responding in under 5 minutes see **391% improvement** in conversion

### The Drop-Off Curve

| Time Since Enquiry | Lead Viability | What's Happening |
|---|---|---|
| < 1 minute | 100% | Customer actively engaged, comparing options |
| 1-5 minutes | 90-95% | Still high intent. May have messaged 1-2 others |
| 5-15 minutes | 60-70% | Losing focus. May have messaged 3-4 competitors |
| 15-30 minutes | 30-40% | Significantly distracted. Likely found another option |
| 30-60 minutes | 15-25% | Most have moved on or been captured by faster competitor |
| 1-4 hours | 5-10% | Weak lead. May have already booked elsewhere |
| 4-24 hours | 1-5% | Almost certainly gone. Responding now feels unprofessional |
| 24+ hours | <1% | Dead. Responding may annoy |

### Missed Call Data

- 62% of consumers who call and don't get through will call a competitor immediately (BrightLocal 2023)
- 75% of callers who reach voicemail will **not** leave a message (Invoca 2023)
- Missed calls cost small businesses an average of £600-1,200/month in lost revenue (CallRail 2023)
- Golden window for returning a missed call: **under 2 minutes**

### Recommended AI Takeover Delays

| Channel | AI Takeover Delay | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Missed call** | **30 seconds** | Customer just heard ringing/voicemail. About to call next Google result. Instant follow-up captures leads that would be permanently lost. 30s gives the phone time to stop ringing |
| **WhatsApp** | **2 minutes** | "Instant" channel — customers expect minutes. 2 min gives owner a realistic window to glance at phone |
| **Instagram DM** | **3 minutes** | Slightly less urgent. Customers in "discovery mode" not "I need this now" mode |
| **SMS** | **2 minutes** | Same urgency as WhatsApp (if SMS is ever added) |
| **Email** | **5 minutes** | Perceived as slower. 5 min still beats 99% of competitors (avg email response: 12 hours) |
| **Facebook Messenger** | **2 minutes** | Same urgency as WhatsApp |
| **After hours (all channels)** | **0 seconds** | Immediate AI response. No one is going to respond at 9pm. Don't waste customer patience |

### Additional Notes

- **Make delays configurable per business.** A solo plumber on-site needs 3-5 min. A salon with a reception desk might want 30 seconds. Default to table above.
- **After-hours = immediate.** No delay outside business hours.
- **Missed calls are the #1 revenue saver.** The 30-second follow-up message is Hey Elsie's killer feature.

## 6. Technical Channel Feasibility

### Phone Calls

**Current setup**: Retell AI + Twilio SIP trunk. Number +447426495169.

**Best option for non-technical business owners: Conditional call forwarding from their existing mobile.**

How it works: The business owner dials a GSM star code. When they don't answer within 15-30 seconds, the call forwards to Elsie's Twilio number.

| UK Carrier | How to Set Up | How to Cancel | How to Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EE** | Dial `**61*[ElsieNumber]*11*30#` or use My EE app | Dial `##61#` | Dial `*#61#` |
| **Three** | Dial `**61*[ElsieNumber]*11*30#` or use My3 app | Dial `##61#` | Dial `*#61#` |
| **Vodafone** | Dial `**61*[ElsieNumber]*11*30#` or My Vodafone app | Dial `##61#` | Dial `*#61#` |
| **O2** | Dial `**61*[ElsieNumber]*11*30#` or use My O2 app | Dial `##61#` | Dial `*#61#` |

These are standard GSM codes that work on every UK carrier including MVNOs (giffgaff, VOXI, Sky Mobile, Tesco Mobile). No store visit needed. Done from phone keypad in 30 seconds.

`*11` = all voice calls. `*30` = after 30 seconds of ringing.

**Alternative: Give the business a new Hey Elsie number.** AI always answers. Transfers to owner's mobile when human needed. Downside: business must update number everywhere.

**Setup complexity**: 1/5 (one star code)
**Cost**: ~£0.10-0.14/min (Retell + Twilio + LLM). UK number rental ~£0.80/month.

**Cost**: Same Unipile account cost

### SMS

**Can Hey Elsie intercept or read a business owner's inbound SMS?**

**No.** This is technically impossible. There is no API, no carrier feature, and no legal way to read SMS arriving on someone else's personal UK mobile number. You would need a separate Twilio number, but UK consumers don't SMS businesses.

**Recommendation**: Exclude entirely.

### Final Pricing: Per-Booking with Monthly Cap

**Model**: Completely free to use. Only charge when the AI books an appointment. Monthly cap. Multi-currency based on user's country (detected by IP at signup).

| Event | UK (GBP) | USA (USD) | Europe (EUR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup | Free | Free | Free |
| AI-answered calls | Free | Free | Free |
| AI-sent messages | Free | Free | Free |
| **AI-booked appointments** | **£20/booking** | **$20/booking** | **€20/booking** |
| **Monthly cap** | **£189** | **$189** | **€189** |

### Rules
- Only AI-created bookings are billable — not manual bookings by the business owner
- Calls, messages, and all other interactions are completely free
- All channels included from day one — no gating, no tiers
- The monthly cap covers all bookings. Once hit, everything is free for the rest of the cycle
- Post-paid billing (like a phone bill) — charged at the end of each 30-day cycle
- 14-day free trial: bookings tracked but not charged, shows "here's what you would have paid"
- Only connect channels the business actually uses (saves Unipile seats)

> "We don't charge you anything. Zero monthly fee. We answer all the calls you miss, 24/7. Any WhatsApp, Instagram, or email you don't reply to within 20 minutes — our AI handles it for you. It knows your business, your prices, your availability. If a customer wants to book, it books them straight into your diary. You only pay when we book you a customer. £20 per booking. Never more than £189/month. That's it."

- **Zero risk for the business owner**: They pay nothing until Elsie delivers actual revenue (a booking)

## 8. Onboarding Flow

### Golden Rule: Value in Under 10 Minutes

SMB SaaS products with "time to value" under 10 minutes have 3-4x higher trial-to-paid conversion than those requiring 30+ minutes (Mixpanel/Amplitude 2024 benchmarks).

### Step-by-Step Flow (~7 Minutes)

| Step | What Happens | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Sign Up** | Email + password or "Sign in with Google". No company details yet | 30 sec |
| **2. Your Business** | Business name, type (dropdown: Hair Salon, Beauty, Plumber, Electrician, Dentist, Other), postcode (auto-fills via Google Places) | 90 sec |
| **3. Teach Elsie** | Pre-populated greeting and FAQs based on business type. E.g. for salons: "Do you do walk-ins?", "What are your hours?", "How much is a cut and blowdry?" Owner edits or skips. Elsie will say "Let me get [Owner] to call you back" for unknowns | 2 min |
| **4. Connect Phone** | "When you can't answer, forward calls to Elsie." Show Elsie's number + simple instructions. Offer "Test it now" button | 90 sec |
| **5. Connect WhatsApp** | Optional. QR code scan via Unipile. "You can do this later" | 60 sec |
| **6. Connect Calendar** | Optional. Google Calendar OAuth. Skippable | 60 sec |
| **7. Test Call** | "Call your business number now and let it ring." Or "We'll call you as a test customer." Show transcript in real-time. **This is the magic moment.** | 60 sec |
| **8. You're Live** | "Elsie is now answering your missed calls. Here's your dashboard." Show inbox. "Your 14-day trial is active." | 30 sec |

### When to Ask for Credit Card

**Day 10 of 14-day trial**, via email:

> "Your trial ends in 4 days. You've had [X] calls handled, [Y] messages replied to, [Z] appointments booked. Pick a plan to keep Elsie working. 3 of those calls came outside business hours — without Elsie, that's 3 missed customers."

### Biggest Drop-Off Points

| Drop-Off Point | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Phone forwarding setup** | In-app video for iPhone and Android. "Need help? We'll walk you through it" button opens WhatsApp chat (on-brand) |
| **No activity in first 48 hours** | Automated email/WhatsApp: "Elsie is ready but hasn't received any calls. Have you set up call forwarding? Here's how." |
| **Trial expiry without conversion** | Read-only downgrade (can see calls Elsie handled, reminding of value). 7-day win-back at 50% off first month |

### Self-Serve with Smart Assistance

- Fully self-serve signup and onboarding (owners want to try at 9pm after closing)
- In-app chat (powered by Elsie — dog-fooding the product)
- Proactive email nudges when they stall
- Optional "Setup call" for Professional tier
