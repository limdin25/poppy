## 1. THE FUNNEL, PAGE BY PAGE

Every screen a buyer sees, in order. The "funnel" is the path from "saw our ad" to "pays us every month."

| # | Page | Price | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Ad** | Free | A short dramatised video clip. An actor re-plays a real property phone call (fake name). Ends on a cliffhanger: *"Here's the exact sentence that got the yes."* |
| 2 | **The Advertorial (bridge page)** | Free | A short story-style article: "The kind of conversation that works." Sits between ad and checkout. Warms the buyer up AND keeps the ad account safe (Meta scans the checkout, not this page). |
| 3 | **The Checkout** | **£27 one-time** | They buy "The Exact Words" — the real recordings + word-for-word scripts. |
| 4 | **The Order Bump** (tick-box on checkout) | **+£17** | "The Objection-Killer Pack" — 11 exact replies to landlord objections. ~1 in 5 add it. |
| 5 | **The Qualifier Question** (one tap) | Free | *"Do you have active property deals right now? Yes / No."* This single question decides the next page. The most important page in the funnel. |
| 6a | **The Upsell — "YES" path** | **£97 first month, then £297/month** | "Investor Edition" — our AI, Elsie, makes the property calls FOR them. The page **plays a live Elsie call before showing the price.** Proof sells it, not the pitch. Community included free. |
| 6b | **The Upsell — "NO" path** | **£39/month** | The Vault community. Small price jump, so more say yes. |
| 7 | **The Downsell** (if they decline 6a) | **£147/month** | "Investor Lite" — Elsie still calls, capped at 150 calls/month. Done-for-you, NOT a cheap self-serve tool (self-serve churns far too fast). |
| 8 | **The Skool Community** | Included above, or £39 standalone | Where they land after buying. Recordings, scripts, weekly live call breakdowns. This keeps them paying. |

**Decisions made:**
- **No free trials anywhere.** Free trials convert at roughly half the rate of paid-first. Paid-first weeds out tyre-kickers.
- **Community bundled into paid tiers**, not sold on top — never compete with ourselves.
- **Cash-pull offers run alongside:** "Founding 50" seats (£197/month locked forever, closes day 21) and an annual option (£2,970, two months free). Pull cash forward when we need it most.

## 2. THE AD PLAN (UK + US)

Everything hangs on one thing first: **does the video stop the thumb?** We test that cheaply before spending real money.

### The 6 angles to test
1. **The Eavesdrop** — "Listen to the call that worked."
2. **The Enemy** — "Agents don't want landlords hearing this."
3. **The Exact Words** — "One sentence flipped a no into a yes. Here it is."
4. **The Mistake** — "If landlords keep saying no, you're opening wrong."
5. **The Number** — "£68k offer on a £105k house. This is the call."
6. **The Silent Proof** — Just a waveform and captions. "Press play, read along."

Shoot **15 versions** by filming one strong call re-enactment and swapping only the first 3 seconds. Start with angles 1, 3, 6 (safest, most specific).

### How many ads, what budget

| Phase | Days | UK/day | US/day | Ads running |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase A — Hook Test** | 1–4 | £150 | £150 | 15 hooks |
| **Phase B — Launch** | 5–10 | £200 | £200 | Top 3–5 winners |
| **Phase B — Scale** | 11–21+ | £350→£600 | £350→£600 | Top 3 + 2–3 fresh/week |
| **Retargeting** | 10+ | £20 | £25 | 1–2 |

UK first (cheaper to learn). Winning UK hooks graduate into the US (US ads cost ~60–90% more, so we don't pay US prices to discover what works).

### How to read the numbers
- **Hook rate** = % who watch the first 3 seconds. This is the gate. Everything else is downstream.
- **CPA** = cost to get one £27 buyer. Starts high (~£50), should fall to ~£30–34 as the system learns.

### Exact kill / scale rules

| Signal | Rule |
|---|---|
| Hook rate **under 25%** after £30 spent | **KILL** that video (by day 2) |
| Hook rate **30%+ (TikTok) / 25%+ (Meta)** | **GRADUATE** it to the buying campaign |
| **Zero** videos pass the gate by day 4 | **STOP. Re-shoot the hooks.** Don't spend on buying ads. |
| CPA **over £45** after £50 spent AND 50+ sales banked | **PAUSE** that ad set |
| CPA **under £40** AND 30+ sales | **SCALE +20% every 48 hours** while it holds |
| Ad set has **fewer than 50 sales banked** | **NEVER cut it for cost.** It needs the data. |
| By day 18, CPA still above £42 | The problem is the **creative, not the budget.** Re-shoot. Don't cut spend. |

**Safety rule (non-negotiable):** public ads only ever show dramatised re-enactments with fake names, framed as education. Real recordings stay locked behind the paywall. Run 3–4 ad accounts from day one so a ban is a 25% dent, not a shutdown.

## 3. THE SKOOL COMMUNITY PLAN

**Decision: ONE combined community for the first 30 days, then split UK/US at week 5.**

A busy room of 400 beats two empty rooms of 200. An empty community makes people leave. Build as one Skool group with country tags, then flip one setting at week 5 to split — no rebuild.

### Seeding (so it never looks dead)
- Before any member arrives, **pre-load 30+ posts** — wins, recording teasers, a pinned weekly schedule.
- Seed with the 3 real proof recordings posted as "wins."
- Founder posts **every day for the first 14 days.** Cheapest retention insurance we have.

### How it converts
- Free members get a **7-day cliffhanger drip** — one recording a day, each cut right before the line that closed the deal. Day 7: *"The full vault + every script: £27, closes in 48 hours."*
- Drip fires across **three channels** — in-app, WhatsApp/SMS, email — because cold members barely open email.
- The £297 upsell gets **three more shots inside the community**: when a member posts a real deal ("Want Elsie to call this?"), live "Elsie booked this" win posts, and a 5-day "watch Elsie work" sequence.

### How it retains (where the money actually lives)
- **First win in 24 hours** — Elsie places a real call for every new Investor member within a day. A customer who has *seen* Elsie work does not cancel.
- **Weekly live call teardowns** (Tuesdays) — the reason not to cancel.
- **3-attempt dunning** before every £97→£297 roll, so failed cards don't quietly kill us.
- **Annual escape hatch** offered to anyone about to cancel.

## 4. THE FREE / CHEAP TRAFFIC PLAN

### The SMS blast — WARNING FIRST
The plan assumes **30,000 property investors**. **This list could not be found in the codebase in the form the plan needs.** What exists there is a ~7,500-row list of scraped US plumbers and home-service landlines — wrong people, wrong country, and texting them cold is likely illegal in the UK.

**Decision: do NOT blast the plumber list.** Before any SMS spend, either (a) confirm/build a real, consented property-investor list, or (b) drop SMS from the 60-day plan and put that money into ads.

*If a real list is confirmed,* the rules: route to **free Skool join, never a cold £27 sale.** Test 2,000 first for ~£180. Only proceed if a test cell clears 2.5% clicks AND 60% real delivery. Realistic: ~£3,800 spend → ~1,000 free members → ~70 buyers.

### Daily content
One strong call recording per week, sliced 15 ways by a dedicated editor: a dramatised ad clip, a talking-head breakdown, a "3 words" carousel, a quote post, a community drop. Post the best 3/day/platform. **10–15 finished assets a week.**

### Email sequence
The 7-day drip is the spine. Plus a **5-day "watch Elsie work"** sequence to everyone who declined the upsell — one real recording per day, soft £97 reminder each time.

## 5. THE VA PLAN

**Two roles, not one.** One person cannot both make calls and turn raw audio into finished videos.

### Caller VA — £240/week (30 hours)
| Block | Time | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Prep | 30 min | Pull call list, review callbacks, load scripts |
| Dial block A | 2 hrs | ~40–50 dials, record everything, tag the tense/closing moments |
| Mid-day | 30 min | Log outcomes, drop the best 1–3 raw clips into the content pipeline |
| Dial block B | 2 hrs | Another ~40–50 dials, same discipline |
| Deal follow-up | 45 min | Chase warm leads, book viewings — where real deals come from |
| Wrap | 15 min | Hand the day's best moments to the editor |

**Daily target:** ~80–100 dials → 8–16 conversations → flag 1–3 genuinely usable dramatic moments.
**Weekly target:** 2–4 great moments + 1 real deal progressed.

### Content Editor VA — £450/month
Turns raw moments + one weekly hero re-record into **10–15 finished assets/week**, keeps a 7-day buffer of ready ad clips so the ad engine never runs dry at day 14.

## 8. THE HONEST ODDS

**Real probability of hitting £10k MRR in 60 days, as designed: roughly 10–15%.**

Three independent reviewers scored it **7%, 15%, and 22%**. The central, most-likely outcome is **£3,000–£7,000 standing MRR by day 60** — a real business being born, but short of £10k on this timeline. £10k is more likely around **day 90–120**.

**Why the odds are low:** the plan needs about seven things to ALL go right at once, and they multiply. Even at generous odds for each, the combined odds land in the teens.

**What must be true for 95% confidence:**
1. **£18–20k working capital genuinely in the bank** — and you do not cut ad spend through the week-4 cash trough.
2. **The ad account survives the first 14 days** (experts put a ban/restriction at 60–70% likely, ~25% terminal).
3. **The hook gate clears** with a scalable winner inside the test budget.
4. **The £97 upsell converts at 7%+** of qualified buyers (could easily be 3–4%).
5. **Month-1 churn stays under 20%**, not the 25–35% plausible on impulse buyers.
6. **A real, legal, consented property-investor SMS list exists** (the current one does not).
7. **All ~20 systems plus the done-for-you calling product ship in week 1–2.**

**The blunt truth: you cannot get to 95% on a 60-day clock.** Several of these — Meta's decisions, how strangers convert, list legality — are *discovered*, not engineered. A genuine **70–80% confidence is reachable, but it needs a 90–120 day frame**, the upsell rate proven on small spend first, and the funnel mostly built before day 1.

## 9. THE ONE THING

**The single biggest risk: the £297 product — "Elsie calls strangers' property deals for them" — does not exist yet, and the whole £10k target rests on it.**

Every other number leans on this one product working. It is the margin engine, the retention spine (the 24-hour first win), and the proof that sells the upsell. Right now we have an outbound agent for *your own* deals, not a service that takes a stranger's deal list and makes calls for them within 24 hours.

**The one thing you must get right:** before spending a pound on ads, build and prove the done-for-you Elsie product on **5–10 real customers**. If Elsie can reliably book a first win in 24 hours for a stranger, the funnel above will carry itself. If it can't, no ad budget, no funnel, and no community will save it.

**Build the product. Prove it small. Then turn on the ads.**
