# Virtual Airbnb Co-Hosting — Business Plan

> **One-line version:** Find landlords worldwide who need tenants, offer to manage their property as an Airbnb co-host at 10% of revenue, handle everything remotely with a VA + AI, and collect automatic Airbnb payouts forever.

---

## 1. What the Business Is

We become the co-host on landlords' Airbnb listings. Airbnb splits the payout automatically — 10% to us, 90% to the landlord. No invoices. No chasing. No tenants. No property visits. The landlord does the one-time setup (keys, cleaner, listing); we run everything after that.

**This is a lead generation business disguised as a property management company.** Once a property is onboarded, it generates passive income. The only job is acquiring more properties.

## 4. Outreach Strategy

### Layer 1 — Referral Agents (primary engine, unlimited scale)

Recruit 10–20 people in India, Pakistan, Bangladesh to act as independent outreach agents. Pay **£50–100 per onboarded property** (only on completion — zero risk). They use their own accounts and IPs, so we never get blocked. Each agent can contact 200–500 landlords per day. 10 agents = 2,000–5,000 approaches per day.

Find referral agents via: Upwork, OnlineJobs.ph, Facebook groups for freelancers.

### Layer 2 — Own Accounts (secondary, runs in parallel)

Multiple accounts across Gumtree, Craigslist, Facebook — each on a different IP (residential proxies or mobile hotspots). Rotate weekly to stay under spam limits.

### Layer 3 — Inbound (builds over time)

- One TikTok / Reel / YouTube Short per week: "We manage your property on Airbnb for free until you earn"
- Facebook group posts in "UK Landlords", "Property Investment UK", etc.
- Airbnb co-host marketplace profile (landlords search for co-hosts here — free leads)

### The Outreach Message (WhatsApp / SMS / DM)

> Hi [Name], I saw your [property/room] listed on [platform]. Have you ever thought about listing it on Airbnb instead of a standard let? We manage the whole thing remotely — guest comms, pricing, check-in — for 10% of what guests pay. You keep 90%. No fees unless you earn. Happy to explain in 2 minutes if you're curious?

Short. One question. No pitch dump.

### Volume & Conversion Assumptions

| Stage | Number |
|---|---|
| Daily outreach (10 referral agents) | 2,000 messages |
| Reply rate | 5% = 100 replies |
| Interested (want to hear more) | 30% of replies = 30/day |

Conservative estimate. Even at half this rate, 12 properties/week = 50 by month 1.

## 5. Landlord Onboarding — The 4 Steps We Ask Of Them

We do everything we can remotely. The landlord does four things once:

1. **Photographs** — They take 15 photos on their phone. We enhance with AI (Luminar AI / Airbnb's own enhancement tool). Professional result, zero cost.
2. **Key setup** — They install a Keynest key safe (~£40 one-time, available on Amazon). We give the code to cleaners and guests via the app.
3. **Cleaner** — We find a local cleaner via Bark.co or TaskRabbit. We coordinate automatically via TurnoverBnB. Landlord optionally meets the cleaner once.
4. **Listing** — We set up the Airbnb listing under their account and add us as co-host (or we create it and transfer). Takes 20 minutes, we walk them through it on a WhatsApp voice call.

**That's it. After those four steps, the landlord does nothing.** We handle all guest messages, check-in codes, pricing, review responses, cleaner scheduling, and payouts.

### How We Communicate With Landlords

- **Onboarding:** WhatsApp voice call (10–15 min) — no Zoom, no video
- **Ongoing:** Monthly WhatsApp message with earnings summary
- **Issues:** WhatsApp — never email

## 6. Operations

### Tools We Use (monthly cost)

| Tool | Purpose | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| PriceLabs | Dynamic pricing (syncs to Airbnb) | £20/property/month (scales down with volume) |
| TurnoverBnB | Automated cleaner scheduling | ~£6/property/month |
| Airbnb co-host | Guest comms, calendar, payouts | Free |
| Guesty / Lodgify (optional, later) | Multi-platform if we expand beyond Airbnb | £200+/month (defer) |

**Total tool cost per property: ~£26/month.** At £180 revenue per UK property, margin per property is ~£154/month before VA cost.

### The VA

One Philippines-based VA handles:
- Guest messages (24/7, using ChatGPT to draft replies fast)
- Cleaner coordination and issue escalation
- Review responses
- New property onboarding admin (listing setup, photos upload, co-host invite)
- Reporting to Hugo weekly

**VA cost: £800–1,200/month (full-time).**

One VA can manage 30–50 properties comfortably. At 50 properties, that's £7,500 gross revenue minus ~£1,300 costs = **£6,200/month profit per VA.**

Add a second VA at 75 properties. Third at 120. The unit economics improve with scale.

### What We Never Do

- Visit properties
- Meet landlords in person
- Hire local staff
- Hold deposits or tenant money
- Sign tenancy agreements

## 9. Channel Manager — Decision

**Do not connect a channel manager in month 1.** Here's the split:

| Phase | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 0–100 properties | **Airbnb only** | Simpler onboarding, no sync issues, Airbnb's own pricing tools are good enough with PriceLabs |
| 100+ properties | **Evaluate Guesty / Hostaway** | Multi-platform (Booking.com, VRBO) adds 20–40% more revenue per property; only worth the complexity at scale |

The channel manager question is really: do we want Booking.com and VRBO traffic on top of Airbnb? Yes — eventually. But the operational overhead of syncing calendars, handling OTA disputes across multiple platforms, and training VAs on multiple dashboards is real. Get to 100 properties clean on Airbnb first, then flip the switch.

## 10. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Airbnb bans co-host accounts | Use multiple Airbnb accounts; co-host model is explicitly supported by Airbnb — not a grey area |
| Landlord churn (stops listing) | Low — once they see monthly payouts they don't stop. Target landlords who've been trying to let without success |
| Bad reviews from poor cleaners | TurnoverBnB tracks cleaner ratings; replace any cleaner below 4.5 stars within 2 bookings |
| Local STR regulations | Airbnb warns hosts about local rules at listing creation; not our legal liability as co-host |
| VA can't handle volume | Pre-defined response templates + ChatGPT drafts; one VA handles 50 properties easily with this setup |
| Seasonal dips | Spread portfolio across UK (urban beats rural), US, AU (opposite seasons) — portfolio-level seasonality flattens out |
